# Glossary map

ha-obsidian-vault-bridge's words come from two glossaries. A term lives in one of them, never both, and this repo never redefines a Pack term.

- **Workflow** (`~/claude-workflow/GLOSSARY.md`, the Pack's): how Josh works with Claude in every repo, such as Stage, Grilling, Spec, UI change, Prototype, Live check, Remote session and the planning words (Family goal, Weekly goal…).
- **Obsidian Helper** (`~/obsidian-helper/GLOSSARY.md`, in the private obsidian-helper repo; this repo has no glossary of its own): the Vault's words and the Bridge's (Secret scan, Held changes and the other safety stops).
